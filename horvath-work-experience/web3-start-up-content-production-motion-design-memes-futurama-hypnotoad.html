Creative Director

Meme reproduction and animation for Futurama classic Fry squinting and a mesmerizing hypnotoad giving you subtle Silent Protocol logo vibes.

While working as Creative Director at Silent Protocol I digitally produced a lot of memes. Most of what is online is a bit below my bar of production quality, so I remake the visuals in Adobe Illustrator before using Adobe After Effects to animate vector paths, add text, and add any VFX before rendering them as animations as video and GIF for social media engaging content.

Fry from Futurama is a classic. It was created before the DAO went pubic about what kind of DeFi tool they were developing — the social media pages were already up an running but a lot of follower didn't actually know who Silent was.

The hypnotoad from Futurama is iconic. If played with sound it leaves a strong impact, highlighting the logo for the web3 developer DAO I was Creative Director for.
