Creative Director

Custom brand meme content levels up production value with animation.

One of my first design requests — many of which contained the meme Pepe the Frog.

This motion graphic was illustrated before the brand, Silent Protocol, went public online. It was meant to highlight how many followers, most of which at the time were crypto influencers or big industry names, were following us before we had even started talking about our product.

I recreated the Pepe the Frog memes in Adobe Illustrator and then animated and added text to the video in After Effects.
