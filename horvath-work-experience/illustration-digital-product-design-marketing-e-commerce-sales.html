Illustrations, Photography, Digital Drawing for society6 Product Sales

Product Designer

Simple black & while illustration for a book of LGBTQ love poems have been selling products consistently every quarter on society6 for over four years now.

This society6 store was filled in 2016 with products covered with illustrations I drew for a book of gay love poetry that I wrote and published. I added a few other images from the time period. The project as a whole was more for myself than anyone else — but then I uploaded a bunch of the files to society6 and created products and was able to sell prints in minutes. To this day, more than four years after setting up the store and, at times, forgetting about it, I reliably sell at least one print every quarter, primarily from a handful of most popular designs.
