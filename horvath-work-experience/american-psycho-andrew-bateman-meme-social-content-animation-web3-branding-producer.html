Silent Protocol Hides Personal Information from American Psychos

Creative Director

Another brand story animation where I started with very little in any form of prompt, made it topical to popular culture and memelords alike, and managed to get the point across in under 30 seconds.

In this production you'll see a visual depiction of users sending currency over blockchain. But they don't know someone nefarious is carefully stalking their every digital finance move. And he's a well known American Psycho, who knows what he might be planning to do with that information. The casual crypto users then deploy the super easy to use Silent Protocol compliancy extension, immediately shielding their personal data from Bateman. He gets just a little frustrated. I digitally drew all art and animated this one on my iPad Pro — that laptop killer has more power than apps to use its full potential ... not that I'd expect Apple to control developers progression into all that processing power.
