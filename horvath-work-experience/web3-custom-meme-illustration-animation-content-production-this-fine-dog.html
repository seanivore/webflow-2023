Mesmerizing Motion Design of Flickering Lights from Surrounding Flames

Creative Director

Flickering fire light animation from the piles of burning fiat cash surrounding Pepe the Frog — but it is okay. This is fine.

A play on the "This is Fine." dog also sitting in a burning house, undisturbed by what is going on around him. In this instance, Pepe is showing us just how fine he is will burning everything around him. He has other plans. Plans that don't involve quite so many pieces of paper.
