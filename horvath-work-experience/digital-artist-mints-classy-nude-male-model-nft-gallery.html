Artist Mints Nude Male Portrait Gallery

Visual Artist + Author

From the human body evoking love to gay aliens — this NFT collection has all the right kind of classy nudity.

Depending on how the dynamic design of this current Webflow UI design is navigating your through my work, you may have seen me praise the Apple Silicon iPad Pro as being an artist, designer, and consultant's perfect laptop killer. Here's another instance.

After getting the Apple Pencil and making these and many more NFTs of digital art, I almost immediately found the best job I've ever had. Strange to be able to identify something so small as a pencil or iPad to being such a catalyst in, by now, making me over $100,000 — while drastically improving my life outlook. An outlook that doesn't appear to ever be fading away again.

My attempt with this collection was to express the soft emotions experienced in the beginning of a relationship. That eventually turned into interstellar homosexuals traveling the galaxy. I suppose a lot of my relationships have progressed in that way.

My absolute favorite pieces are the abstract ones that consist of a collection of seemingly random colored shapes, all slightly deconstructed from the form. This, perhaps obviously, represents the feelings towards the end of a relationship.
