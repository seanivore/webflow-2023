Digital Animation Design for Branding web3 Start-up

Creative Director

Vector Drawings of Brand Product Marketing Animations of Metaphors for the DAPP

In the start of my Creative Director role at Silent Protocol, I was kept in the dark about the details of their web3 privacy dapp in development. With creative time on my hands I went to task finding different ways to show privacy. Using the no evil emoji as inspiration, I drew this digital vector art on my iPad in Adobe Fresco. I moved it over to the iOS version of Adobe Illustrator to create curve objects for aspects of the illustration that I was going to give motion. Then I moved to my Macbook Pro to animate the vector paths in Adobe After Effects.

Much like this money, using Silent Protocol means no one can see, hear, or speak about your activities on web3.
