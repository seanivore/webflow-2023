Brand Product Purpose Translated Visually Using Character Storytelling

Creative Director

Our "privacy is so secure" it matches the security necessary to hide the aliens living in Area 51 since they landed.

The brand, a web3 privacy protocol developer called Silent Protocol, in looking to start an ongoing conversation about privacy and the role it plays in society.

The first posts of this goal are very top level — simply finding ways to illustrate privacy, in a way that will be enjoyable and maybe funny to the viewer. The humor reaction, our gut check, is what is necessary to hook in the viewer to take action, whatever that may be in the context of the post. Here we have a private area where individuals can interact anonymously.

Because of its privacy, it has in inherent exclusivity. And so aliens that landed in area 51 all those decades ago are still living there, protected by privacy provided by our brand. The brand character, a monkey who shows up throughout their digital presence, has made friends with his VIP guests.

I digitally drew all the image using Adobe Fresco, and I have prepped the storyline for easy animation into video content in the future.
