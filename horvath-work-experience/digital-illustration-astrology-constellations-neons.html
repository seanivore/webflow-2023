Simple, Digital Drawings of the Animals Represented in Zodiac Constellations

Producer, Digital Media

Outline and filled vector drawings, colorized on-trend with an extra splash of neon. I almost went with two male twins for Gemini, then instead opted for adding a male Virgo.

The 12 zodiac constellations popular in modern astrology and the animals that represent them are: Aries (Ram), Taurus (Bull), Gemini (Twins), Cancer (Crab). Leo (Lion), Virgo (Maiden), Libra (Scales), Scorpio (Scorpion), Sagittarius (Archer), Capricorn (Goat), Aquarius (Water Bearer), Pisces (Fish).

These are simple drawing on a tablet.
