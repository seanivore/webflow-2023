Meeting the Development Team by Drawing Them in Anime

Creative Director

Adding another layer of anonymity to our remote workplace, I illustrated all of our core team, primarily developers and cryptographers, as avatar characters from the anime show Black Clover.

This was my first project at Silent Protocol two years ago. It was an great way for me, the only creative of a staff of developers and technologists, to meet everyone.

The quickest of the team also saw the value in showing camaraderie by uploading the avatars to their Discord profile, where we conducted virtually all our work. As employees weren't pushed to use the avatars, it was a uniquely quick way to identify those with a bit of a stubborn mind and perhaps still lingering a bit too long in a hipster phase; at worst a full on narcissist. Thankfully that was not the case, as the Silent Protocol group are some of the nicest people you'd ever be lucky enough to meet. Though you will need to travel to at least five different countries around the world to meet them all face-to-face.

Oh and bonus: the first batch of images are merely fun, art versions of the completed avatar versions in the second batch of images.
